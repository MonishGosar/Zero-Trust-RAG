# Northstar Quarterly Report

This is a fictional document for testing the RAG pipeline. All figures below are synthetic.

## Revenue by region

| Region | Revenue | Year-over-year growth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| APAC | $82M | 12% |
| EMEA | $65M | 8% |
| Americas | $102M | 10% |

Total quarterly revenue was $249M. APAC growth was driven by enterprise contracts.
EMEA growth came primarily from renewals. Americas growth came from expansion deals.

## Expenses

Operating expenses were $171M. Research and development accounted for $54M, sales and
marketing for $72M, and general and administrative expenses for $45M.

## Customer metrics

Northstar ended the quarter with 1,420 enterprise customers. Net revenue retention was
118%. Customer satisfaction was 91%. Annual logo churn was 4%.

## Plans

The next-quarter priority is improving onboarding. The onboarding team owns the initiative,
and the target is reducing time to first value from 14 days to 7 days.

## Risks

The primary risk is delayed enterprise procurement. The mitigation is earlier security reviews.
No full-year revenue guidance or headcount forecast is provided in this report.
